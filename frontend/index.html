From brief to payout, in three moves.

1

Post the brief

A brand sets category, platform, follower floor, engagement floor and budget. Beam scores every creator on the board against it out of 100 and ranks them.

2

Pitch and agree

Creators see the full budget and their own match score before they apply. Brands move each application through review, then accept the ones they want.

3

Deliver and get paid

Accepted work moves to in progress, then completed. Both sides watch the same status, so nobody chases an invoice or a screenshot.
